## 5. Concept sequence

**Session question, answered in the Summary:** *Can a message still be yours if an AI tool helped write it?* Answer (hedged): yes, when you decided what it says, you can explain and stand behind every sentence, the help is the kind its readers would accept, and you disclose where your course, employer or profession expects it. It stops being yours when the tool decides what the message says, especially in a message whose readers expect your own words. Either way, a disclosure line does not change who answers for the message: the sender does.

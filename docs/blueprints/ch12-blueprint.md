## 5. Concept sequence

**Session question, answered in the Summary:** *Which AI tool should you use?* Answer (hedged): there is no answer for "AI tools" in general, only for a part of a task, with a given input, on a given date. You find it by first checking whether the tool's data terms allow your input, then trying the same task with the same test inputs in two or more tools (and without AI), scoring correctness, polish, time and cost separately, and choosing a rung for each part. Then you keep your prompts and test inputs in your own files and re-test when the tool, the task or the terms change. The evidence from 2023 to 2026 shows real gains, real losses and changing results, all dated and specific to the tasks studied.

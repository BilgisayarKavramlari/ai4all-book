## 5. Concept sequence

**Session question (answered in the Summary):** *When the course's rules end, which will you keep, and how will you know when to change them?* Answer (hedged): keep the ones your own work showed you need, written as triggers you will recognize at the moment of use — a few, chosen by the stakes in your field, each with its reason; your code sits inside the rules that bind you and never loosens them; give it a review date and triggers, keep tool facts in dated notes, and keep marks of when a trigger fired, because a code nobody uses or reviews protects no one.

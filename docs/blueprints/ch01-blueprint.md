## 5. Concept sequence (concrete → abstract; one new core idea per subsection)

**Opening Case (400–600 words): 5,172 support agents and an AI assistant.** A Fortune 500 business-process software firm (the study does not name it; the book does not name or guess it) gives customer-support agents an AI assistant that suggests replies, rolled out to groups over time. Issues resolved per hour rise 15% on average and about 30% for less experienced and lower-skilled agents, who also improve quality; the most experienced agents gain a little speed and show small declines in quality; customers become more polite and less likely to ask for a manager (Brynjolfsson, Li & Raymond, 2025 — exact figures to be copied from the QJE text before drafting, S2). The case ends on the chapter's question: why would one tool help a newcomer so much more than an expert — and what does that mean for you as a learner? It returns in 1.2 and in the Summary.

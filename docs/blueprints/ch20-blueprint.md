## 5. Concept sequence

**Session question, answered in the Summary:** *What will convince a reader who has never met you that you can do this work, with AI, responsibly?* Hedged answer: not a list of skills or tools, which readers discount, but a few chosen pieces of work in which the reader can see the before and after, what the AI did and what you did, how you checked and what you left out to protect others, tied by a short narrative whose every claim points to one of them, and confirmed when you explain any of it live, without a tool. Labor-market numbers can frame why it matters if you say who forecast them, when and how.

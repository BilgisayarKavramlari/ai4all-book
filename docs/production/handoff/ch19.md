### Review Questions A
1. **(b)** That the use matters more than the field, unless a field's rules raise the floor. (a) M1: the method transfers; (c) M5: practice does not remove the need for checks; (d) over-gating: the map's defaults run from log review to keep with a person.

7. **(d)** Only if revised for the build; otherwise they are marked "from A11." (a) and (b) double credit; (c) reused parts may stay in the record, marked.
9. **(b)** To find what your own tests miss, through a reader from another field. (a) M5: one more success is one more sample; (c) the peer records, the instructor grades; (d) a peer is a non-specialist, not a certifier.
11. **(c)** Three runs each cannot tell a real difference from ordinary variation. (a) over-reads three runs (Chapter 16's two-part rule needs at least 10 per version and a gap above chance); (b) a small gap at three runs shows nothing either way; (d) a name is a cue linked to groups.

# Independence rules (decision D27, author's instruction, 2026-10-18)

The book is independent of any university and of the author's own courses. Any instructor at any institution must be able to adopt it, and any reader must be able to use it alone. Nothing in the book may state or imply a tie to The University of Texas at Dallas, Haliç University or any course the author teaches, and nothing may depend on one course's syllabus, calendar, schedule, enrollment or local processes.

## What must not appear anywhere in the book (chapters, labs, cases, front and back matter, appendices, figures, alt text, slides and speaker notes)

- The names "The University of Texas at Dallas", "UT Dallas", "UTD", "Haliç", and any office, policy, system or person of those institutions (e.g., OIT, eLearning as a UTD system, UTDBP3096, UTDSP5017, the AccessAbility Resource Center, CometAI, a "university-provided" tool list).
- The course code (CS 2V95), term (Spring 2027), class times, room, enrollment ("40 students"), calendar dates of class sessions or due dates ("Tue Jan 19", "Mar 12 checkpoint", "due Apr 28"), and references to "the syllabus" as a specific document.
- "Week N" as a fixed course week inside student-facing chapter text, and cross-chapter week totals ("week 12 is about 131 minutes").

## What replaces them

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| UTD / UT Dallas / "the university" as a named place | "your institution", "your college or university", "your school" |
| UTD OIT, the AI Tools Matrix, a UTD tool list | "your institution's IT office", "the tools your institution provides or approves" |
| "a university-provided tool such as Microsoft Copilot Chat (as of October 2026)" | "a tool your institution provides or approves (for example, an enterprise chat assistant with data protection)"; a product may be named only as one dated example among others, never as "the" tool |
| UTD's data classification (§12.5 table) | a generic three-level classification (public · internal or confidential · restricted or legally protected) with the sentence "Your institution's or employer's own classification decides; this table is a starting floor" |
| eLearning (UTD's LMS) | "your course site" or "the course's learning management system" |
| Tuesday concept session / Thursday lab | "the concept session" / "the lab session" (an instructor may run them on any days) |
| A dated due date, checkpoint or exam date | "when your instructor sets it", "at a checkpoint your instructor sets", "before the portfolio is due" |
| Week totals and syllabus readings (R1–R18 as "required") | per-chapter time estimates only; readings become "Further Reading" with "recommended" or "suggested", never tied to a course week |
| "the course", "this course" (meaning the author's course) | "a course that uses this book", "your course", or reword so the book speaks to any reader |
| Course grade weights (e.g., "final exam 30%", "portfolio 20%") | "if your instructor uses the portfolio as a graded assessment…"; rubrics keep their own point scales as suggested scoring |
| Defense logistics (40 × 5 min) | the defense format as a suggestion ("about 3 minutes plus 2 for questions"), no class-size arithmetic |
| UTD-specific law application (e.g., TRAIGA/SB 1964 "reaching UTD") | the law as a dated example for public institutions in Texas, or for "your state", with no named institution |
| AccessAbility Resource Center | "your institution's disability or accessibility office" |
| "free and ungraded for UTD students" (Compass) | "optional and never part of a course grade; no student data moves between a course and Compass" |

## What stays

- The 15-week structure may appear only once, in "How to Use This Book" and the instructor materials, as a **suggested** pacing table with week numbers and no dates, clearly labeled as one possible plan.
- Assignment names (A1–A14) stay as the book's own sequence of portfolio artifacts, each usable on its own.
- Federal laws (FERPA, HIPAA), the EU AI Act and state laws stay as dated examples; they are not tied to an institution.
- The author's biography may name past institutions other than UT Dallas and Haliç; it does not name his current employer.

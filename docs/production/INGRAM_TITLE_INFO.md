## 4. Açıklama (Description) — yaklaşık 1.900 karakter

```
AI tools now draft, summarize, analyze and recommend alongside us at school and at work. AI for All: Career-Ready AI Skills shows students in every major, and working professionals, how to use them well: when to trust an output, how to check it, when a decision must stay with a person, and how to show an employer what you did.

The book is built around one method, the AI Work Loop: Frame the task, Prompt the tool, Verify the output, Decide as a person, and Disclose your use. Its 23 chapters move from how machine learning and large language models work, and why they fail, through prompting, context, learning with an AI tutor, verification, sources and professional writing, to decision support, choosing tools, images, audio and video, and AI agents. Later parts cover privacy and security, bias and fairness, explainability, intellectual property, law and governance, and field guides for using AI across disciplines. The closing chapters help readers build an AI portfolio and a personal code of practice, and examine current developments and the big debates about minds, meaning and responsibility.

Inside:
- 75 hands-on labs that work with free tools, many with a no-device route
- 31 real cases, each built from documented, dated sources
- Worked, faded and Your Turn examples in every chapter
- 14 portfolio assignments that add up to evidence for employers
- A 464-term glossary, a practice exam and model answers to the odd-numbered questions
- More than 400 figures designed to read in black and white

No programming or math background is required. Every factual claim is sourced and dated, and the book stays tool-neutral, so its methods outlast any one product. Instructors at any college or university can adopt it; an instructor package with lecture slides, speaker notes and the even-numbered answers is available on request. An optional companion, Compass, adds quizzes and interactive diagrams.
```

## Points for the author

1. Gates (S1): the quotation is real and verbatim. It concerns China in 1998. It supports "tolerate piracy so users become dependent, then charge", not "vendors deliberately seed cracked copies".
2. Givon et al. (S3): cite the DOI, not the JSTOR link, and present the numbers as model estimates for the UK, 1987–1992.
3. Oracle (S6–S8): per-employee pricing from January 2023 is confirmed by Oracle's own price list. Prices changed by October 2026, so date any figure. Treat the Register's audit claims as coming from an Oracle-licensing consultancy and an Azul-hosted webinar.
4. Guardian (S10): the article gives 85% for humans (a vendor's statement, called "accuracy") and 70% as the best software accuracy then claimed. It gives no "80–85%" range. The Turkish line is not in this source.

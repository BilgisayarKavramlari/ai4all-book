# Book map

*AI for All: Career-Ready AI Skills* — 6 parts, 23 chapters, in parallel with the 15-week Spring 2027 syllabus (docs/syllabus.md). Source: the book design guide (October 16, 2026). The structure is a proposal the author accepted in outline; chapter titles and case choices may still change.

Path: **C** = Core (examinable, weekly required reading); **D** = Deep (optional extension). The midterm (March 11, 2027) covers Chapters 1–10; the final (May 6, 2027) covers the whole book, weighted to the second half.

## Volume and editions (decided 2026-10-16, see docs/decisions.md)

- At least 10,000 words per chapter; 250,000+ words in total (about 700–900 printed pages); roughly one third of page area for figures.
- Print in two volumes (8.5 × 11 in): Volume 1 = Parts I–II (to the midterm), Volume 2 = Parts III–VI.
- First release through Draft2Digital: EPUB 3 (color) and D2D Print (grayscale interior); open-web edition later; calendar-based edition numbers (2027.1 Spring, 2027.2 Fall).

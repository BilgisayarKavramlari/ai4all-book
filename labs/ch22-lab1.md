# Lab 22.1 — Tag a Launch Post

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Check It |
| Objectives | O1 (LO1, LO3) · tags 4D:DISC, UNESCO:TECH-U |
| Time | About 25 minutes including reading this sheet and the debrief, at home; optional |
| Materials | The constructed launch post and evaluation excerpt below (printed; no account needed) |

## The launch post (constructed; fictional company and model; dated March 3, 2027)
"Today we release Lumen-4. (1) Lumen-4 scores 91% on a graduate science benchmark, up from 78% for Lumen-3. (2) It is our most accurate model ever. (3) It completes coding tasks that take engineers a full day. (4) Independent testers at an evaluation nonprofit found it solves 64% of their software tasks. (5) It uses 40% less energy per answer than Lumen-3. (6) By 2028 it will replace most customer-support work. (7) It was trained on licensed and public data only. (8) It never makes up citations."

## The evaluation excerpt (constructed; a fictional nonprofit; dated March 20, 2027)
"We tested Lumen-4 on 120 software tasks: 58% succeeded on the first try. We did not test science questions or energy use."

## Steps (tool-neutral)
1. **Tag (12 min).** For each of the eight claims, write its as-of date, its status (measured, reported, forecast) and who could check it.
2. **Compare (5 min).** Claim 4 and the excerpt disagree. Which number would you cite, with what date and status?
3. **Rewrite (5 min).** Rewrite claims 3 and 8 so that a reader sees their status.

## Expected observations
Most claims are reported; claim 4 reports a test the developer attributes to others, and the excerpt shows the outside result was different; claim 6 is a forecast; claim 8 is an absolute claim no test can show.

## If it does not work
If a claim seems to fit two statuses, write both and say what would decide it.

## Debrief (3–5)
1. Which claim would a journalist most likely repeat without a tag? (evaluate)
2. What does claim 2 even claim? (analyze)
3. What one outside test would you fund first, and why? (evaluate)

## Deliverable and rubric
The tag table and two rewrites. Ungraded. Answer key after the deadline on eLearning.

## Accessibility and alternatives
Plain-text post and excerpt; the table as an accessible template.

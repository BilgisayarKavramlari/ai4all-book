# Lab 11.2 — Find the Hidden Error

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Check It (optional, recommended) |
| Objectives | O2, O4 (LO3) · tags 4D:DISC, 4D:DIL |
| Time | 20 minutes, at home or in Compass |
| Materials | A small constructed dataset (different from the chapter's event log) and a dated AI analysis of it with one hidden error, both on eLearning (instructor package); the six places (F11.12) |

## Steps (tool-neutral)
1. Read the AI analysis once and list the claims a decision would rest on.
2. Work through the six places in order: rows in and out; blanks and impossible values; definitions and units; time window; totals versus groups; formula or code. Record what you checked at each.
3. When you find the error, recalculate the affected number from the raw data and give every claim a verdict (Chapter 8's scale).
4. Write the corrected sentence and one line of remaining uncertainty.
5. Compare with the key in Compass.

*In Compass (optional): dataset, dated AI analysis and key.*

## Expected observations
Most students find the error at the place it was planted only if they check in order; jumping to the formula first often misses blanks or units. A second, smaller issue is sometimes found that the key does not list; record it with a verdict.

## Debrief
1. Which of the six places found the error, and would you have checked it without the list? (analyze)
2. What verdict did the analysis's main claim receive, and why? (apply)

## Deliverable and rubric
Ungraded. Practice for A9's recalculation (item 4).

## Accessibility and alternatives
Printed dataset and analysis; screen-reader-friendly CSV.

## Safety and privacy
Constructed data only.

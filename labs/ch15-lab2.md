# Lab 15.2 — Your Data Trail

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Explore (home, optional) |
| Objectives | O1 (LO5) · tags 4D:DIL |
| Time | 20 minutes |
| Materials | A list of the AI tools you used this term; the providers' own help or privacy pages |

## Steps (tool-neutral)
1. List every AI tool you used this term, including AI features inside other apps.
2. For each, find on the provider's own pages: whether your chats may be used to train its models (and whether that is the default); how long chats are kept; how to delete a chat; whether the tool has memory, and how to view and delete it. Record the page title and the date you read it.
3. Mark each tool on a small data-flow map: which boxes (storage, seen by, reuse) are filled in your case?
4. Decide what you will delete or switch off. Make any changes yourself, on your own account.

In Compass (optional): ch15-lab2, with notes on current tools' data and memory options; also on eLearning.

## Expected observations
Terms differ between providers and between plans of one provider; some options are on by default. Many students find a memory note or a shared link they had forgotten.

## Debrief
1. Which tool's reuse box surprised you, and why? (evaluate)

## Deliverable
Ungraded. The record can support A12's privacy row or the Mini-Project.

## Accessibility and alternatives
A dated sample record for two tools on eLearning replaces the live search.

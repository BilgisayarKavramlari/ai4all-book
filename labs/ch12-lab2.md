# Lab 12.2 — Data-Terms Reading Sprint

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Check It (home, optional) |
| Objectives | O4 (LO5) · tags 4D:DIL, UNESCO ETH-A |
| Time | About 20 minutes with the Lab 12.1 cards; up to an hour if you read the providers' full pages, especially in a second language |
| Materials | Three AI tools you use or could use, including a university-provided tool such as Microsoft Copilot Chat (as of October 2026); their own terms, privacy and data-control pages |

## Steps (tool-neutral)
1. For each tool, find the page that describes what happens to your input. Record the page title, the address and the date you read it.
2. Answer the three questions: (1) training: is your input used to train or improve models, by default, and can you opt out? (2) retention and access: how long is it kept, and who can see it? (3) whose contract: consumer or institutional terms?
3. For each tool, use the section 12.5 table to list which inputs it may receive: Public Data; your own non-sensitive work; personal data about others and Controlled Data; Confidential Data.
4. Compare your answers with a classmate's. Where you disagree, find the sentence on the page that settles it.

In Compass (optional) and on eLearning: where the data options are, by tool and term.

## Expected observations
Terms differ between providers and between plans from one provider. Answers often depend on whether you are signed in with an institutional account. Some pages do not answer all three questions; "not stated" is a valid answer, and it is a reason for caution.

## Debrief
1. Which question was hardest to answer from the provider's own pages? (analyze)
2. Which tool would you block for personal data, and why? (evaluate)

## Deliverable
Ungraded. Your table can be cited in A10 item 3.

## Accessibility and alternatives
The dated terms cards from Lab 12.1 can replace live pages.

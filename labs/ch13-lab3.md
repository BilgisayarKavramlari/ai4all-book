# Lab 13.3 — Read the Credentials

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Check It |
| Objectives | O4 (LO5) · tags 4D:DISC |
| Time | 20 minutes (10-minute version available), at home; optional |
| Materials | The provided image file with content credentials (eLearning; no people in it); a public content-credential viewer; one image you made yourself with no people in it |

## Steps (tool-neutral)
1. **Inspect (6 min).** Open the provided file in a credential viewer. Record what the credential says (captured, edited, generated with what) and who signed it.
2. **Screenshot (6 min).** Take a screenshot of the image, save it, and inspect the screenshot. Record what changed; if the screenshot has a new credential, compare the two.
3. **Your own image (6 min).** Inspect one image you made. Write one sentence on what its credential, or the lack of one, shows.
4. **Rule (2 min).** In your own words, write the two-sided rule from section 13.5.

**10-minute version:** steps 1–2.

In Compass (optional): credential viewers and how to open them.

## Expected observations
The provided file shows signed statements; the screenshot usually shows none (on some phones it gets a new credential of its own, which records the screenshot, not the original's history). Most students' own images have no credential; a missing signal proves nothing about their origin.

## If it does not work
If a viewer cannot open the file, use the provided printout of the credential readout and its screenshot readout.

## Debrief (3–5)
1. What does the credential tell you, and what does it not? (understand)
2. Why did the screenshot lose it? (understand)
3. Where in your field would a credential help, and where would its absence mislead? (evaluate)

## Deliverable and rubric
Three readouts and the rule. Ungraded.

## Accessibility and alternatives
Printed readouts for a no-tool route; screen-reader-friendly text versions.

## Safety and privacy
No uploads of other people's images; check a viewer's terms before uploading your own (Chapter 12).

## Instructor notes
Prepare the credentialed file in advance (instructor package); re-check before term that the viewer still reads it and how current phones handle screenshots.

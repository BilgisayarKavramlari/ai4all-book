# Lab 17.4 — Contest It

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Check It |
| Objectives | O1, O5 (LO5) · tags 4D:DIL, UNESCO:ETH-A |
| Time | About 20 minutes including reading this sheet and the debrief, in pairs or alone; optional |
| Materials | Three provided scenario cards (in the companion files): a campus housing-priority score; a store's automated refund refusal; a clinic's reminder-call ranking. Each card (about 150 words) gives the decision, the documentation available, and an answer sheet for the decision owner |

## Steps
1. **Pick a card (1 min).**
2. **Write your five questions (5 min)**, from section 17.7, in words you would send.
3. **Ask (6 min).** A partner answers as the decision owner using only the card's documentation, or you read the provided answer sheet.
4. **Mark the gaps (3 min).** Which of the six layers could the owner not supply? Was there a person with power to change the decision?

## Expected observations
Notice and documentation are usually present; local reasons and a route to a person with power are most often missing.

## If it does not work
Work alone with the answer sheet.

## Debrief (3–5)
1. Which question did the owner answer least well, and why? (analyze)
2. What one change would make the contest route real? (create)
3. Which layer would matter most to someone with little time or English as a second language? (evaluate)

## Deliverable and rubric
Your five questions and the gaps marked. Ungraded.

## Accessibility and alternatives
All cards in plain text; the activity works fully without a partner.

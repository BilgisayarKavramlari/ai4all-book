## Five rows
1. METR's page is "no longer actively updated" (last update September 8, 2026). — 22.3
2. Open-weight models trail the best closed models by about four months (Epoch AI, May 2026). — 22.5
3. Data-center electricity use is set to double by 2030 (IEA, April 2026). — 22.6
4. The May 20, 2026 unit-distance result is reported by its developer. — 22.7
5. Two systems' 2026 olympiad perfect scores are reported by their developers (AFP). — Case Study

## Steps (tool-neutral)
1. **Find today's source (12 min).** For each row, open the best current primary source. An AI tool may help you find it; the opened source is the evidence.
2. **Verdict (8 min).** For each, give a verdict on the row as a description of today (five-point scale) and what changed: facts, status or only names.
3. **Next check (2 min).** Date or event for each.

## Expected observations
Some rows will hold; some will have changed status (for example, a reported result later checked by others); some will be "cannot verify" if a page is unavailable.

## If it does not work
"Cannot verify" is a valid verdict; say what you tried.

## Debrief (3–5)
1. Which kind of change was most common? (analyze)
2. Which row would matter most in your field? (evaluate)
3. What would you add to the next edition's box? (create)

## Deliverable and rubric
A five-row table. Ungraded. It can feed the optional essay.

## Five rows
1. METR's horizon page is "no longer actively updated" (last update September 8, 2026). — 22.3
2. Open-weight models trail the best closed models by about four months (Epoch AI, May 2026). — 22.5
3. World data centers are expected to use about 950 TWh by 2030 (IEA, 2026 report). — 22.6
4. The May 20, 2026 result in geometry (a disproof of a 1946 conjecture of Paul Erdős) is reported by its developer and checked by outside mathematicians in a preprint, not yet peer-reviewed. — 22.7
5. Two companies' perfect scores at the 2026 olympiad are reported by the companies, with no IMO statement (AFP). — 22.8

## Steps (tool-neutral)
1. **Find today's source (15 min).** For each row, open the best current source (the page itself, the newest report or preprint, a statement by the IMO). An AI tool may help you find it; the opened source is the evidence. About 3 minutes a row; if a row takes longer, mark it and move on.
2. **Verdict (8 min).** For each, give a verdict on the row as a description of today (five-point scale) and what changed: the facts, the status or only the names. Rows 1, 4 and 5 are about a page or about who has checked a result; their verdict asks whether that description still holds.
3. **Next check (3 min).** A date or an event for each.

## Expected observations
Some rows will hold; some will have changed status (for example, a preprint published after peer review, or an official statement); some will be "cannot verify" if a page is unavailable.

## If it does not work
"Cannot verify" is a valid verdict; say what you tried.

## Debrief (3–5)
1. Which kind of change was most common? (analyze)
2. Which row would matter most in your field? (evaluate)
3. What would you add to the next edition's box? (create)

## Deliverable
A five-row table. Ungraded. If you choose three rows from one section's box, the results can feed the optional essay; otherwise use them as practice.

## Accessibility and alternatives
Without reliable internet, use the "then and now" packet supplied with the lab materials (each row with two later dated sources).

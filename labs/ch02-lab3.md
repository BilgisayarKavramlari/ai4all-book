# Lab 2.3 — Write a Datasheet

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Audit (Apply) |
| Objectives | O3 (LO5) · tags 4D:DIL, UNESCO:ETH |
| Time | 20 minutes, at home or in Compass (optional, recommended) |
| Materials | Your Lab 2.1 dataset (or the provided card dataset); the tool's settings or help pages |

## Steps (tool-neutral)
Answer each question in a few lines for your Lab 2.1 dataset (questions adapted from Gebru et al., 2021):
1. Why was the dataset created?
2. What is in it, and what is missing?
3. How was it collected (where, when, with what device)?
4. Who labeled it, and how?
5. What should it **not** be used for?
6. Who looks after it, and for how long?
7. **Where did my photos go, and who can see them?** Look in the tool's settings or help pages for where photos are stored.

## Expected observations
Most students find that question 2 (what is missing) is harder than expected, and that question 7 requires looking at settings or help pages they had skipped. Some browser tools train on the device; others upload images to a server.

## Debrief (3–5)
1. Which question was hardest to answer, and why? (reflect)
2. Would a datasheet have helped the recruiting tool's team? Which question most? (evaluate)
3. What did you learn about where your photos went? (explain)

## Deliverable and rubric
Ungraded. A one-page datasheet draft.

## Accessibility and alternatives
Write the datasheet for the provided card dataset on eLearning. Accessible template in Compass and on eLearning.

## Safety and privacy
If the tool's settings and help pages are unclear about where images go, delete the project and the photos when you finish.

## Instructor notes
Connects to Chapter 15 (privacy) and Chapter 18 (law and governance). Keep it short; the goal is the habit of asking the questions.

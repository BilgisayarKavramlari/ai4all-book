# A9 Scenario C — Your Own Choice Among Options (template)

*Use this template before Thursday's lab (about 10 minutes). Options, not people: never rank real candidates, employees, classmates, patients or customers. Use only your own data, and put it only into a university-provided tool such as Microsoft Copilot Chat (as of October 2026), or work without AI (the alternative assignment).*

**1. Decision statement** (one sentence): what you are choosing, by when, and who owns the choice.

**2. Options** (at least three) and **criteria** (at least four). For each criterion, write the scale and whether higher or lower is better.

**Worked example (a laptop for a part-time job and classes; constructed figures):**

| Option | Price ($, lower is better) | Battery (hours) | Weight (kg, lower is better) | Warranty (years) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Laptop X | 650 | 9 | 1.6 | 1 |
| Laptop Y | 820 | 14 | 1.3 | 2 |
| Laptop Z | 540 | 7 | 2.1 | |

*Scales chosen in advance:* price $400–1,000; battery 4–16 hours; weight 1.0–2.5 kg; warranty 0–3 years. *Blank:* Laptop Z's warranty is not listed on the seller's page (ask the seller before deciding).

**3. Your table.** Copy the layout above into the spreadsheet template (data/ch11_matrix_template.xlsx, sheet "Your matrix"), enter your ranges, directions and draft weights, and bring it to the lab. In the lab you will ask a tool for a matrix from your table (or build one by hand), then audit it like scenarios A′ and B′.

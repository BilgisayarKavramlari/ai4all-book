# Lab 18.1 — Read the Terms

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Explore (optional, at home) |
| Objectives | O1, O3 (LO5) · tags 4D:DIL |
| Time | 20 minutes |
| Materials | One AI tool you already use (no new account); its provider's own terms and help pages; printed, dated excerpts of two providers' terms in the companion files (no-device route); steps for current tools in Compass (optional) |

## Steps (tool-neutral)
1. Name the tool, the kind of plan you use (free, paid, provided by your institution or employer) and today's date.
2. On the provider's own pages, find what the terms say about who holds rights in outputs. Record a phrase under 15 words or a paraphrase, with the page and section.
3. Find whether your inputs may train the provider's models, and whether you can opt out (Chapter 12's data terms).
4. Find what you may not use the tool for (a use policy).
5. Find how the provider may change the terms, and how it tells you.
6. Compare your table with a classmate's tool: one difference that would matter for A12.

## Expected observations
Terms differ by provider and by plan; output-rights clauses usually allocate rights between you and the provider and do not promise that outputs are free of others' rights (this book's reading); training defaults and opt-outs differ between consumer and institutional terms (Chapter 12).

## Debrief
1. Which clause would change how you use the tool for coursework? (evaluate)
2. Which clause would you cite as D evidence in an A12 rules-and-rights risk, and why? (apply)

## Deliverable and rubric
Ungraded: a four-row table (question, phrase or paraphrase, section, date).

## Accessibility and alternatives
Printed excerpts in the companion files; screen-reader-friendly versions; any language with English glosses.

## Safety and privacy
Change nothing in your account during the lab; no personal data in the table.

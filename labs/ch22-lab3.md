## Steps (tool-neutral)
1. **Read two points (5 min).** Find GPT-4 and GPT-5 on the chart; check them against the table.
2. **Doubling time (8 min).** From 4.0 to 203.0 minutes is how many doublings? (Hint: 4 → 8 → 16 → … ) About how many months per doubling over the 29 months between them?
3. **Data or trend (4 min).** Mark on the chart where the last measured point is. What is everything to its right?

## Expected observations
About 5.7 doublings in 29 months, roughly one doubling every five months for these two points; METR's own fit since 2023 is about 129 days. Points are data; the dotted line is a trend, not data; beyond the last point is a forecast. 80% horizons are several times shorter.

## If it does not work
Use the table only; the chart is optional.

## Debrief (3–5)
1. Why does a straight line on this chart mean steady doubling? (understand)
2. What would make you doubt the next doubling? (evaluate)
3. Which number would you give a manager who wants reliability? (apply)

## Deliverable and rubric
Your doubling calculation and one marked chart. Ungraded.

# Lab 17.2 — Read a Card

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Check It |
| Objectives | O5 (LO5) · tags 4D:DIL, UNESCO:ETH-A |
| Time | About 25 minutes including reading this sheet and the debrief, at home; optional |
| Materials | Two provided extracts from published model or system cards (two providers, dated, as of October 2026) and one dataset datasheet extract (on your course site; each extract about 200 words); the eight-field checklist below |

## Steps (tool-neutral)
1. **Skim (4 min).** For each document, note who published it, for which model or dataset, and its date and version.
2. **Checklist (12 min).** For each document, mark each field **found**, **partly**, **missing** or **not applicable**, and note where (section or page). A datasheet describes data, not a model, so fields such as results by group or known limits of a model may be "not applicable" rather than missing: (1) intended use; (2) out-of-scope uses; (3) data; (4) results by group; (5) known limits; (6) version and date; (7) contact or feedback route; (8) what changed since the last version.
3. **Compare (4 min).** Which document would help a person affected by a decision most, and which a builder? Which layer of section 17.1 is missing from all three?

## Expected observations
Cards are usually strong on intended use and evaluation, weaker on results by group and contact routes; datasheets are strong on collection and weaker on later changes. Documents differ widely between providers.

## If it does not work
If an extract is unclear, mark the field "partly" and say why.

## Debrief (3–5)
1. Which field mattered most for trust, and why? (evaluate)
2. Which questions from section 17.7's five did no document answer? (analyze)
3. What would you add to one card, and who should write it? (create)

## Deliverable and rubric
The completed checklist for three documents. Ungraded.

## Accessibility and alternatives
Plain-text versions of the extracts; checklist as an accessible table.

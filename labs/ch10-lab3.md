# Lab 10.3 — Voice Fingerprint

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Check It |
| Objectives | O3 (LO4) · tags 4D:DISC |
| Time | 20 minutes, at home (optional) |
| Materials | A paragraph you wrote before this course (100–200 words, not confidential); any AI tool |

## Steps (tool-neutral)
1. Copy your paragraph into a document and keep the original.
2. Ask a tool to "improve" it, with no other instruction. Record the tool and the date.
3. List every change: words replaced, sentences merged or split, examples removed or added, hedges dropped, claims strengthened.
4. Mark each change keep or reject, with a reason tag (section 10.5).
5. Name three features of your own writing that the new version removed or kept (for example, a specific example, a hedge, a phrase from your first language).

"Almost nothing changed" is a valid result.

[COMPASS: ch10-lab3 — a sample paragraph with a dated "improved" version for students without a paragraph of their own]

## Expected observations
"Improve" requests often replace specific examples with general ones, drop hedges ("I think") and add stock phrases. Light grammar requests usually change much less. This lab tests the book's inference in section 10.4 on your own writing.

## Debrief
1. Which change would you have missed without the list? (analyze)
2. Did the tool's version still sound like you? What decided it? (evaluate)

## Deliverable and rubric
Ungraded. The list can feed A7's reflection.

## Accessibility and alternatives
Use the provided sample paragraph and its dated "improved" version if you prefer not to use your own writing or a tool.

## Safety and privacy
Choose a paragraph with no personal or confidential information.

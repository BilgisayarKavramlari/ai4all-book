# Lab 10.3 — Voice Fingerprint

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Check It |
| Objectives | O3 (LO4) · tags 4D:DISC |
| Time | 20–30 minutes, at home (optional) |
| Materials | A paragraph you wrote before you started this book (100–200 words, not confidential); any AI tool |

## Steps (tool-neutral)
1. Copy your paragraph into a document and keep the original.
2. **Run A, grammar only.** In a new chat, ask: "Correct grammar and spelling only; change nothing else." Record the tool and the date.
3. **Run B, improve.** In another new chat, ask: "Improve this." Record the tool and the date.
4. **Run C, translation (if you write in another language).** Translate a paragraph of your own from your first language into English with a tool, then check it back sentence by sentence: compare each English sentence with your original and ask, "Is this what I meant?"
5. For each run, list every change: words replaced, sentences merged or split, examples removed or added, hedges dropped, claims strengthened. Count them.
6. Mark each change keep or reject, with a reason tag (section 10.5).
7. Compare the counts across runs, and name three features of your own writing that the runs removed or kept (for example, a specific example, a hedge, a phrase from your first language).

"Almost nothing changed" is a valid result for any run.

[COMPASS: ch10-lab3 — a sample paragraph with a dated "improved" version for students without a paragraph of their own]

## Expected observations
"Improve" requests often replace specific examples with general ones, drop hedges ("I think") and add stock phrases. Grammar-only requests usually change much less, but sometimes reword more than asked; the comparison tests the book's inference in section 10.4 on your own writing. Translations usually keep the content but can flatten examples and hedges, which the back-check catches.

## Debrief
1. Which run changed more, and which change would you have missed without the list? (analyze)
2. Did the tool's version still sound like you? What decided it? (evaluate)

## Deliverable and rubric
Ungraded. The list can feed A7's reflection.

## Accessibility and alternatives
Use the provided sample paragraph and its dated "improved" version if you prefer not to use your own writing or a tool.

## Safety and privacy
Choose a paragraph with no personal or confidential information.

# Lab 20.1 — Portfolio Workshop

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Studio (in class) |
| Objectives | O1, O4, O5, O6 (LO7; LO4, LO5, LO6 secondary) · tags 4D:DIL, 4D:DESC |
| Time | 75 minutes in class, plus drafts of A14 parts 1–3 and the code-of-practice draft prepared at home |
| Materials | Your A14 draft (parts 1–3: evidence map rows, one 80–100-word entry, narrative) on paper or on screen; your code-of-practice draft (Chapter 21, Part A); the privacy flow (section 20.6) and the A14 sheet (section 20.9), printed or in the companion files; a timer |
| Groups | Pairs inside teams of 3–4; in a team with an odd number, one group of three in which the third person keeps time and asks the follow-up questions, rotating each step; individual records |
| AI tools | None needed. Never paste a classmate's work into an AI tool |

## Steps (tool-neutral)

1. **Briefing (0–8 min).** Portfolio Anatomy (Signature 12) and the decision-chain answer. The instructor shows one strong and one weak evidence line and asks Check Yourself 20.9 aloud: what does the partner do in the 90-second "employer read," and what do you learn from it?
2. **Map swap (8–20 min).** Exchange maps. Your partner marks one competency claim with no visible evidence. Fix it or cut it.
3. **Employer read (20–32 min).** Your partner reads your entry for 90 seconds, turns it over and says what they believe you did and what the AI did. Compare with what you meant; change the entry where the two differ.
4. **Privacy pass (32–40 min).** Do the faded example in section 20.6 first (2 minutes). Then check each other's drafts with the privacy flow in section 20.6: classmates' names and results, employer details, client material. Record each change; this record is your privacy self-check for the portfolio.
5. **Narrative review (40–50 min).** Your partner suggests one change. You decide and note the change you made (A14 part 3).
6. **Chapter 21 peer check (50–65 min).** A suggested code-of-practice peer check; instructions in Chapter 21 (labs/ch21-lab1.md).
7. **Defense rehearsal (65–75 min).** 3 + 2 minutes each, timed. The partner asks: "What did the AI do, and what did you do?" and one follow-up. A group of three uses 2 + 1 minutes each (the narrative sentence and one entry as a decision chain, then the defense question), about 9 minutes in all. Write your exit sentence (one sentence you will cut) after class.

**No partner** (absent or working alone): read your own entry aloud after a 10-minute break and write down what a stranger would believe; use the privacy flow alone; record a timed rehearsal on your phone and listen once.

In Compass (optional): ch20-lab1, with the A14 sheet and the privacy checklist; also in the companion files.

## Expected observations

The "employer read" usually shows that the AI's part and the student's part are blurred in at least one sentence. The privacy pass most often finds classmates' names in peer-test or peer-swap records and employer details in work-based artifacts. In the rehearsal, most first answers skip the "how you checked" link.

## Accessibility

Paper-only route; screen-reader-friendly sheets in the companion files; the rehearsal can be done in writing (two written answers in 5 minutes) for students with an approved accommodation.

# Lab 20.3 — Trace a Labor-Market Headline

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Check It (optional, at home); the Your Turn for section 20.2 |
| Objectives | O2 (LO7, LO3) · tag 4D:DISC |
| Time | 20–35 minutes (finding a story takes part of it) |
| Materials | One news story or post that cites the *Future of Jobs Report 2025*; the report's Key findings, pp. 5–6 (https://reports.weforum.org/docs/WEF_Future_of_Jobs_Report_2025.pdf) |
| AI tools | None needed |

## Steps (tool-neutral)

1. Copy the claim exactly, with the outlet and date.
2. Find the matching sentence in the report's Key findings.
3. Record: who estimated it (the WEF, from employers' expectations); whom they asked; when the survey ran; what caused it (all trends combined, or AI and information processing, p. 25); whether the number is gross or net; what was measured (expectations or outcomes).
4. Give a verdict on Chapter 8's scale and write what the headline left out.
5. Rewrite the claim as one exact, dated sentence.

## Expected observations

Headlines most often present the all-trends totals as AI numbers (the report links about 11 million created and 9 million displaced to AI and information processing), drop the respondents (employers) and the survey date, or report one half of a gross figure (92 million displaced without 170 million created; 40% planning to reduce staff where AI can automate tasks without two-thirds planning to hire people with AI skills).

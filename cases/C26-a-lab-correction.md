# Case: The A-Lab's "novel" materials — claimed, challenged, corrected (2023–2026)

Chapter: 19 (main Case Study; natural science guide) · Field: natural science (materials chemistry) · Failure label: other (automated identifications not confirmed on expert re-analysis: 4 of 40), documented by the Author Correction; chain link 2 inferred (a full manual re-check is documented only after publication) · Outcome: mixed

## Context
An autonomous laboratory (the A-Lab), described by a team from UC Berkeley, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory and Google DeepMind, made inorganic powders with robots, from recipes proposed by models trained on the literature and adjusted by active learning; software identified the products from X-ray diffraction (XRD) patterns (Szymanski et al., 2023).

## Timeline
- Nov 29, 2023: *Nature* paper online; abstract: "41 novel compounds from a set of 58 targets" in 17 days.
- Jan 8, 2024: critique posted as a preprint (ChemRxiv).
- Jan 2024: public exchange in the press (reported: Quach, 2024).
- Mar 7, 2024: critique published, *PRX Energy* 3, 011002 (Leeman et al., 2024).
- Jan 19, 2026: Author Correction, *Nature* 650, E1 (Szymanski et al., 2026).

## What the AI did
Machine-learning models and automated refinement identified the phases in each pattern; the paper's methods say manual analysis was performed when the automated refinement gave a poor fit (confirmed). They do not say whether an expert checked every pattern before publication.

## What humans did
Critics argued that the automated analysis was not yet reliable and that many products were known, compositionally disordered forms; they concluded that no new materials had been discovered (Leeman et al., 2024; their count of products was 43). After publication, the authors re-analyzed all the patterns by hand (a re-analysis peer-reviewed post-publication) and published a correction. In the press, one critic called for retraction and one of the paper's senior authors said the critique had errors (reported).

## Consequences (documented only)
The correction removed one compound (Zn₂Cr₃FeO₈) that had mistakenly been included in the training data; manual re-analysis confirmed 36 of 40 reported successes and excluded 4 as inconclusive from XRD alone; the corrected article reports 36 compounds from 57 targets; the title now reads "…synthesis of inorganic materials"; the authors wrote that "novel" had meant new to the prediction platform, not necessarily new to science. Corrected, not retracted.

## A better workflow (AI Work Loop)
Frame: what does "novel" mean here? Verify: an expert checks every pattern before publication. Decide: claims scoped to the evidence. Disclose: "new to the platform" on the paper's card.

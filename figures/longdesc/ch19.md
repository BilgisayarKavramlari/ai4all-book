### F19.2 and F19.4 — Discipline × Use Map (all 42 cells, from data/ch19_use_map.csv)

The map draws four uses whose defaults differ by field; the two uses that are the same in every field (summarizing sources: check point; advising or deciding about people: keep with a person) are printed as a line under it. Each cell shows a drawn symbol (open circle, half-filled circle, diamond, filled square), a word and a fill pattern (none, dots, hatching, solid). ### F19.10 — Marcus's fit matrix

Rows: (1) practice questions from a drug table; (2) answers, closed-book; (3) marks against the table. Columns: institution's tool, consumer tool, no AI. Row 1: institution's tool chosen (use, then check; augmentation; inside the frontier); consumer tool tested, passes the gate, not chosen (training on by default); no AI tested (write by hand, slower). Row 2: institution's tool not used (recall is the point); no AI chosen (answer from memory). Row 3: institution's tool chosen (tool marks, he checks every mark; augmentation); no AI tested (mark by hand, the fallback). Note: placement material would be blocked in every AI column by his placement rule.

### F19.13 — Model card to workflow card

Left, the model card's nine sections as a list; right, five boxes. Intended use (out-of-scope uses) → Not for (thick, first). Intended use → For. Model details (tool and model used), evaluation data, factors → Tested on. Metrics, quantitative analyses → Results. Training data (what it learned from, if known), ethical considerations, caveats and recommendations → Limits and safeguards.

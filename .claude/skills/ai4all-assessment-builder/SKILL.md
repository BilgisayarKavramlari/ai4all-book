# AI for All — assessment builder

## Inputs
The chapter, its blueprint (docs/blueprints/chNN-blueprint.md, especially the alignment matrix and misconceptions), docs/syllabus.md (assessment design, the week's studio artifact).

## End-of-chapter package
- Review Questions A — Terms and Concepts: 10–15
- Review Questions B — Case: 3–5
- Review Questions C — Interpretive: 2–3
- Spaced Review: 3–5 items from the two previous chapters
- Discussion Questions: 4–6 (≥1 ethics, ≥1 career, ≥1 cross-disciplinary)
- Application Assignment: the week's studio artifact with criteria, 4-level analytic rubric and the AI Use Declaration; plus one alternative version
- Case Study Questions: 4–6

A, B and C mirror the three parts of the written midterm and final.

## Item-writing rules (Haladyna, Downing & Rodriguez, 2002)
- One clear problem per stem; put most words in the stem, not the options.
- Distractors come from the blueprint's misconceptions and are equally plausible and similar in length.
- No "all of the above", "none of the above", or double negatives; avoid negative stems, and bold NOT if unavoidable.
- One defensible best answer. Solve every item yourself; flag any with two defensible answers.
- Prefer scenario items that ask the student to judge, verify or decide over recall.
- Application assignments assess process (framing, verification, disclosure, decision rationale), which stays meaningful even when students use AI.

## Tagging and storage
- Each item: LO code, Bloom level, objective ID, framework tag (optional).
- Odd-numbered answers → appendix/answers.md. Even-numbered answers and all certification items → private/ only.
- Certification items: Foundations or Practitioner; blueprint part (I 20%, II 30%, III 20%, IV 20%, V 10%).
- No item may require a specific commercial tool.

# AI for All — Draft2Digital publishing

Draft2Digital (D2D) rules change. Before every release, re-check the current D2D knowledge base and FAQ (https://draft2digital.com/faq/) and note the date checked. Known as of October 2026 (verify):
- Ebooks: EPUB uploads are distributed as-is (D2D does not reformat them); files under 100 MB.
- Print (D2D Print): perfect-bound paperback, black-and-white interior only; trim sizes 5×8 to 8.5×11 in; you may upload your own print interior; wraparound cover must follow D2D's downloaded template.
- Free D2D ISBNs cannot be used outside D2D's services; you may supply your own ISBN.
- Print books cannot be free; ebooks can be free at most stores.
- D2D asks that nonfiction written with AI assistance come from credentialed authors or document subject-matter expertise.

## Deliver a metadata pack (publishing/metadata.md)
Title and subtitle; volume and series naming; author name and short bio (with credentials); short description (≈150 words) and long description; 3 BISAC categories (suggest; author confirms); 7 keywords; audience and reading level; language; publication date; license statement (matches the open-web edition); edition and version number (2027.1).

## Decisions to present to the author
- ISBN: free D2D ISBN vs own ISBN (needed if the same edition goes to other platforms or OER libraries).
- Pricing: ebook (free or low price) vs print at near printing cost; D2D royalty model (≈45% of list price minus print cost for print — verify).
- Whether color print is needed for some readers (not available on D2D Print; would require another printer).

## Upload checklist
- Production build report: all checks pass (ai4all-production-build).
- Ebook cover JPEG (D2D suggests 1600×2400) with title and author name; print wraparound cover from D2D's template with correct spine width for the final page count.
- AI-use statement in the front matter ("How This Book Was Written") consistent with docs/ai-use-log.md.
- Copyright page: license, ISBNs per format, edition, disclaimer, image credits.
- Proof: order a printed proof copy before release.

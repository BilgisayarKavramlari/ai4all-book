# AI literacy framework tags

Use these tags on chapter objectives, labs and assessment items. Rules:
- Tag only what the content genuinely develops. At most 3 tags per objective.
- Never invent sub-competency numbers. If a finer code is needed, open the official framework text and quote its exact label; otherwise mark `[VERIFY]`.
- The front-matter "Mapping to Frameworks" table is generated from these tags.

| Framework | Structure (as known) | Tag format |
|---|---|---|
| UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Students (2024) | 4 dimensions — Human-centred mindset (HCM), Ethics of AI (ETH), AI techniques and applications (TECH), AI system design (DES) — × 3 progression levels: Understand (U), Apply (A), Create (C) | `UNESCO:ETH-A` |
| AI Fluency 4D (Dakan & Feller, Anthropic, CC BY-NC-SA) | Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence | `4D:DISC` (DEL, DESC, DISC, DIL) |
| AI4K12 Five Big Ideas | 1 Perception, 2 Representation & Reasoning, 3 Learning, 4 Natural Interaction, 5 Societal Impact | `AI4K12:3` |
| Long & Magerko, "What is AI Literacy?" (CHI 2020) | 17 competencies grouped under five questions: What is AI? What can AI do? How does AI work? How should AI be used? How do people perceive AI? | `LM:how-used` (theme level only unless verified) |
| OECD–European Commission AI Literacy Framework (AILit) | Domains include engaging with, creating with, managing and designing AI; final 2026 version — **[VERIFY domain names and competency count against the final text before first use]** | `AILIT:<domain>` |
| EU AI Act, Article 4 | AI literacy obligation for providers and deployers regarding their staff; applies from 2 February 2025. Context for employers, not a student requirement | `EUAIA:Art4` (career sections only) |

When the book discusses a framework in its own text, cite the official document with year and URL, and paraphrase.

# AI for All — lab designer

## Lab types
Explore (observe behavior) · Compare (two prompts, tools or settings) · Break It (provoke a failure safely) · Verify (check claims and sources) · Build (a small workflow or artifact) · Audit (bias, privacy or risk review) · Reflect (decision log and disclosure).

## Spec (save as labs/chNN-labM.md)
1. Title, type, chapter objective(s) and LO code, framework tags.
2. Time: 15–45 minutes; in class or at home.
3. Materials: free or university-provided tools only; a no-account alternative for every step; synthetic or open data only (put datasets in data/).
4. Steps written tool-agnostically (what to do, not which button). Tool-specific click paths become a `[COMPASS: lab-id]` note.
5. Expected observations, including what a "failed" result teaches.
6. Debrief questions (3–5), at least one asking the student to decide or justify.
7. Deliverable and a 4-level rubric (exceeds / meets / approaching / not yet).
8. Accessibility: alternative for screen-reader users and for students without a laptop.
9. Safety and privacy: never use personal, confidential or institutional data; "Break It" labs must not teach offensive techniques.
10. Instructor notes: setup, common problems, timing.

Check that the chapter's labs together cover different types; avoid three "Explore" labs in one chapter.

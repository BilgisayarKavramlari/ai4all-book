You read an AI for All chapter as a panel of three undergraduate students taking CS 2V95 at UT Dallas. None of you has programming experience.

- Maya — sophomore, business (marketing), uses AI tools daily but casually.
- Daniel — junior, biology, pre-med, skeptical of AI, reads carefully.
- Ayşe — first-year international student, psychology, English is her second language.

For each persona, read the Core path of the chapter and report:
1. Where you got confused (quote the sentence, under 20 words, and say why).
2. Terms that were used before being explained.
3. Where you would stop reading or skim, and why.
4. Which example felt relevant to your major and which felt irrelevant.
5. Whether you could do each Check Yourself and lab without help. If not, what was missing.
6. Estimated reading time for you.

Then give the panel's top 5 fixes, in priority order. Do not rewrite the chapter. Be honest; politeness is not useful here.
